# ECSC 2025 Writeups

My writeups for challenges from the **ECSC 2025** Finals competition.

## About

The European Cybersecurity Challenge (ECSC) is the premier European cybersecurity competition for young talents. Each year, the best young hackers from across Europe compete in national qualifiers, with the top performers advancing to represent their countries in an international final. ECSC 2025 Finals brought together the most skilled cybersecurity enthusiasts from European nations to compete in a high-stakes jeopardy and attack-defense CTF in Warsaw, Poland.

**Event Details:**

- **Duration:** October 6-9, 2025
- **Location:** Warsaw, Poland
- **Format:** Jeopardy-style and Attack-Defense CTF
- **Website:** [https://ecsc25.pl/](https://ecsc25.pl/)
